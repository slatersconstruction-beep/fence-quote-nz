How sending works
  • WhatsApp: tap Share PDF → WhatsApp → pick the contact → Send. The PDF arrives as a document. If Share is not offered, tap Download PDF, then in WhatsApp open the chat → + (iPhone) or 📎 (Android) → Document → pick the file from Downloads/Files.
  • Email: tap Share PDF → Mail or Gmail. A draft opens with the PDF already attached; add the client's address, check it and tap Send yourself. Email draft (no attachment) only opens a pre-written message: you must attach the downloaded PDF yourself.
  • Clients just open the PDF. No account or link to this app is needed.

Estimating assumptions

My trade rules of thumb used for the takeoff. They are not Building Code requirements.

    When to get a site-specific design

    • Over 2.5 m high (also needs building consent).
    • High wind exposure, coastal cliff-tops, open rural sites.
    • Soft, filled, wet or sloping ground; rock.
    • Pool barriers, fences on retaining walls, fences at a fall/drop, acoustic or fire walls.

    Data & devices

    • Quotes, accepted records, your business profile and price overrides are stored only on this phone, inside this browser (browser local storage). Nothing is uploaded to a server; the website only delivers the app.
    • Safari and the Home Screen app keep separate storage on iPhone. Pick one and stick with it (Home Screen is recommended).
    • Switching phones or browsers: tap Export backup, keep the file (e.g. email it to yourself or save to Drive/iCloud), then Import backup on the new phone.
    • Clearing website data, uninstalling the browser, or iOS removing data from an unused site will delete records. Export a backup regularly; also keep the accepted PDFs you send.
